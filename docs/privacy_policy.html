Privacy Policy

Quiet Prayers · Effective date: October 27, 2026

In short: Quiet Prayers does not collect your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own phone. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. About the app

Quiet Prayers (“the app”, “we”) offers short prayers and Bible verses to read one at a time, with optional personal touches such as your first name and the names of people you would like to remember or pray for.

2. Information stored on your device

To personalize the app, you may choose to enter the following. It is saved only on your device:

  • your first name (optional);
  • your age range, and answers to the short questionnaire (for example, what is hardest right now);
  • names of people or pets you would like to remember or pray for, and how you know them;
  • prayers you saved, the days you prayed, and your reading streak;
  • your settings (text size, theme, background music on or off, reminder time). The calm background music is generated on your device; no audio files are downloaded.

We do not receive, see, or store this information on any server. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Erase my data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share personal data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • The app does not need an internet connection to work.

4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is created on your phone and sent by your phone at the time you choose. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone settings.

5. Sharing and copying

When you use “Share” or “Copy”, the prayer you selected (as text or as a picture) is handed to the app or person you choose. We do not see where it goes.

6. Device backups

Your phone’s own backup service (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include the app’s data, according to your device settings. That is controlled by your device and your account with that provider, not by us.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Not professional advice

Quiet Prayers offers words of comfort and reflection. It is not medical, mental-health, legal or professional advice. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number right away. In the United States you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

9. Scripture and content

Bible verses are from the King James Version (public domain). The prayers in the app are original.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, when the change is important, mention it in the app’s release notes.
