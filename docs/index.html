Common questions

Where are my answers and saved prayers kept?
Only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

How do I change my name or my answers?
Open the gear icon (Settings) and tap Edit my answers.

How do I make the text bigger?
Settings → Text size → A++.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?
Settings → Daily reminder. The reminder is set on your phone; no server is involved.

How do I erase everything?
Settings → Erase my data, or uninstall the app.

What is Quiet mode?
The moon icon hides everything except the prayer and keeps the screen on while you read.

About

Quiet Prayers offers short original prayers with a verse from the King James Bible (public domain). It has no ads, no account and no tracking. The words are for comfort and reflection; they are not medical or professional advice. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number (in the United States, call or text 988).
